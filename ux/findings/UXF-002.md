Docusaurus hides the navbar's colour mode toggle at widths up to 996 px and shows it only inside the
opened menu. At 390x844 a reader switches theme in two actions, opening the menu first.

Frequency: every page at 390x844. Cost: S (one rule in `website/src/css/custom.css`).

Fix: the toggle stays in the navbar at every width.

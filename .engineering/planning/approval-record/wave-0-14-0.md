## Constraints

- U2 fixes the drain inside `ingress_bridge` and leaves the documented `InputEndpoint::recv`
  contract as it is.
- On 2026-11-05 the coordinator approved, under its grant, one additive public method for U2: a
  provided method on `InputEndpoint` that answers without waiting whether the edge is known to be
  empty, known to hold something, or unknown. Its default answers unknown, which keeps the
  behaviour before it; `QueueReceiver` and `SinkReceiver` implement it, and the ingress bridge
  trusts only a known answer. Reasons given: a provided method with a default breaks no
  implementer, no caller and no documented contract; 0.14.0 is a minor release; it meets the
  story's acceptance instead of narrowing it. Documenting that `Pending` from `recv` means empty
  was refused, because it narrows the contract. The method is named so that it cannot collide with
  a likely implementer method, and `CHANGELOG.md` names it under Added. The bound on extra polls
  for a receive that wakes itself stays for endpoints that answer unknown.
- The `ess` pin moves to 0.86.0 only once the coordinator reports that release verified (done; see
  ESS pin).
- story:ess-defect-fixes stays out of the wave until the coordinator decides.

## Acceptance of the admitted-send unit

The coordinator set the terms for story:run-ingress-admitted-send-outlives-the-drain on
2026-11-05, after each of its three corrections opened a new defect: a given-up send handed into
the queue was still delivered; a reserved envelope could be taken before its send's next poll;
and a drain could wait forever on a send its caller held unpolled. Correction 3 is the unit's
last, and it is accepted on tests, not on a reading of its diff:

1. A case for the held send, written the way a caller would write it: the send polled under a
   timeout that fires while it waits, then the run's drain awaited, then the send awaited. The case
   is red on `f54cb70` and green on correction 3.
2. Every adversary case from passes 1 and 2 green.
3. The threaded give-ups under `pause` and `drop_stale_epochs` deliver no given-up send (the base
   showed 0 of 206 and 0 of 274), and the repeat loops show no failures.

If all three hold, the unit merges and the release commit and the gate follow. If any case is
red, or any earlier case reopens, the unit leaves the wave:

- `queue.rs` and `drive.rs` stay at their pre-unit state on the integration branch;
- the release ships the other two units;
- the story returns to active with a Design section (the send and drain state machine, written
  down before any code) for a later wave.

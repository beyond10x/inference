## Decision

The coordinator approved step 1 of this wave on 2026-11-05, under the orchestrator's grant for
waves, commits, pull requests and releases through the bot route.

Step 1 writes the send and drain state machine for
story:run-ingress-admitted-send-outlives-the-drain into the story's Design section, with the
chosen design: the bridge closes its edge to senders in one step, through an additive provided
method on `InputEndpoint` whose default keeps today's behaviour, the same shape as
`known_contents`. Step 1 is one store commit on `wave/0.15.0`, with no build.

The coordinator accepts that choice in principle, because it meets the story's five requirements
and keeps the plain-node delivery case green. The final accept follows its reading of the
section.

Step 2, the implementation, starts only after that accept, and only once the orchestrator's
managed trees hold under 20G of build output together. It runs under these terms:

- 2G of `target/` and 20 cargo minutes, told at 15;
- one adversary pass, then at most one correction;
- accepted on the tests the Design section names: a red case, or an earlier case reopened, takes
  the unit out again.

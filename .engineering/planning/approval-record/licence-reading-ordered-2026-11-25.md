## Decision

On 2026-11-24 the operator decided, for the owner: release 0.34.0 "is not tagged before
`story:sherpa-onnx-linked-libraries-licences-read` is done for every library the released
archives link", and "Reading licence texts is in your reach; a licence that forbids what we ship
is a stop and comes to me." On 2026-11-25 the operator ordered the reading to start: "the licence
reading starts now (one read-only agent); no tag until wave 2 is merged and the reading is in."

The coordinator moved story:sherpa-onnx-linked-libraries-licences-read from draft to proposed to
active on these words.

## What this does not authorise

No tag and no release. A library whose licence the policy does not admit is not decided here: it
goes to the operator as a decision of its own, with what carries it. The reading changes no file
of the repository; recording its result in the two adapters is the story's unit and follows the
reading.

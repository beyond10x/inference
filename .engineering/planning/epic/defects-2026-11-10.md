## Context

On 2026-11-10 the repository had 0 open GitHub issues (`gh issue list -R beyond10x/inference`
printed nothing). The planning store held eight draft stories that describe wrong behaviour on
`main` at `af93222a`, most filed from adversary and security findings of the SDK waves and of the
0.14.0 wave. This epic holds them so that one wave fixes them and one pull request integrates them.

## Stories

- story:cascade-turn-spans-in-a-burst
- story:docs-build-without-warnings
- story:duplex-turn-ids
- story:live-session-send-after-its-own-turn
- story:signed-url-token-copies
- story:tool-authority-schema-keywords
- story:waiting-sends-land-in-the-order-they-started-waiting
- story:withdrawn-ingress-send-gives-back-its-sequence

## Not in it

- story:sdk-usage-cost: a feature of `epic:sdk`, and it `depends_on` story:sdk-one-call, a draft
  whose handles (`crates/inference/src/chat.rs`, `transcriber.rs`, `voice.rs`) are one- and
  two-line stubs on `main`.
- story:audio-device-adapter and story:realtime-voice-demo: accepted, and what remains of each is
  the operator's run on a laptop with a microphone and a speaker.
- story:ess-defect-fixes: accepted, blocked by dependency-blocker:ess-affects-witness (open).

## Acceptance

Every story above is implemented, or has left the wave with its reason recorded in its body, and
the wave's one pull request is merged into `main` with `task check` and the shared gates green.
